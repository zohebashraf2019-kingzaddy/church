Welcome to Saint Michael Parish

Continuing the healing presence of Christ in the North Valleys

Called to collaborative ministry in virtue of Holy Baptism, the people of God in Saint Michael Catholic Community continue the healing presence of Our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ in the communities of the North Valleys by celebrating and sharing the Divine Will with all peoples. — Our Mission Statement

We are a diverse, bilingual parish of the Diocese of Reno, located two blocks from the Stead Airport. Under the pastoral care of Rev. Elberto Meléndez, we gather each week in English and Spanish to worship, serve our neighbors and grow together in faith.

Coming Up

    Sacraments

    Milestones of grace

    For any sacrament, please call the parish office at least a few weeks ahead so we can help you prepare.

    Baptism

    Welcome your child into the family of God. Parents and godparents complete a short preparation class.

    Reconciliation

    Confessions are heard Saturdays at 3:30 PM, or by appointment with Father.

    First Communion & Confirmation

    Children and teens prepare through our Religious Education program. Director: Susan Walker, (775) 737-9337.

    Marriage

    Contact the office at least six months before your wedding date to begin marriage preparation.

    Anointing of the Sick

    For the seriously ill, elderly or those facing surgery. In an emergency, call the office right away.

    Becoming Catholic (OCIA)

    Adults interested in the Catholic faith, or adults seeking Baptism, Communion or Confirmation, are warmly invited.
